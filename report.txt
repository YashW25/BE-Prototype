1. Abstract

Legal, law-enforcement, and investigation departments handle large volumes of sensitive documents including First Information Reports (FIRs), investigation reports, witness statements, forensic reports, charge sheets, evidence records, court documents, legal notices, and judgments.

Traditional paper-based systems and fragmented digital storage can create difficulties related to document retrieval, unauthorized access, document modification, version management, collaboration, and auditability.

NyayaVault is proposed as a secure, centralized and role-based digital document management platform designed for legal and investigation workflows.

Prototype 1 focuses on establishing the foundation of the system through:

Secure authentication
Role-based access control
Case management
FIR management
Secure document storage
Cryptographic document hashing
Document integrity verification
Audit logging
Role-specific dashboards
Police-station/FIR integration simulation

The prototype uses Supabase Authentication, PostgreSQL and Storage as the initial cloud infrastructure and is designed for deployment using Vercel.

Blockchain functionality is represented in Prototype 1 through an integrity-record abstraction/mock ledger so that the complete workflow can be demonstrated without requiring a production blockchain network. A future version will integrate a permissioned blockchain such as Hyperledger Fabric.

Law enforcement agencies, courts, legal departments and investigation organizations require secure management of sensitive documents.

Existing approaches can result in:

Difficulty locating documents.
Unauthorized access.
Risk of document tampering.
Lack of reliable version control.
Fragmented information.
Inefficient collaboration.
Limited auditability.
Difficulty verifying document integrity.
Manual handling of FIR and case information.

A secure digital platform is therefore required to manage documents throughout their lifecycle while maintaining confidentiality, integrity, availability and accountability.

15. Document Integrity

The prototype uses SHA-256 hashing.

When a document is uploaded:

Document
   ↓
SHA-256
   ↓
Unique Hash

Example:

Document Hash:

9a2c7e83c8f1......8f31

The hash is stored as part of the document integrity record.

When verification occurs:

Stored Hash
      │
      │ Compare
      ▼
Current Document Hash

If:

Stored Hash == Current Hash

the document is considered unchanged relative to the registered integrity record.

24. Limitations of Prototype 1

The prototype intentionally has limitations.

1. No real government integration

The FIR service is simulated.

2. No production blockchain

Blockchain is represented by an integration abstraction/mock ledger.

3. Limited AI

Advanced OCR and semantic search are not part of Prototype 1.

4. Prototype storage

The storage architecture is suitable for demonstration but requires additional security controls for production deployment.

5. No legal certification

The prototype demonstrates technical integrity mechanisms and should not be represented as legally certified evidence-management infrastructure.
